Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Causal is a spatial canvas for thinking. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

01Who we are

Causal Software Limited is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 17280800. For anything to do with privacy, email contact@causal.so.

We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer, so privacy questions go to the address above.

For everything described here we act as the data controller, meaning we decide what is collected and why. If we later offer Causal to organisations for their teams, we would act as a processor for the content those teams put in, under a separate agreement. We will update this policy before that changes.

02A note on your content

Most of this policy is about account and usage data. But the thing you probably care about most is what sits on your canvases. So we will state our position up front.

Your canvases belong to you. We do not read, mine, sell, or publish the content you put into Causal. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train machine learning models.

Our staff do not access the contents of your canvases except in these narrowly limited situations:

  • You ask us to. For example, you report a bug and give us permission to look at the affected canvas so we can reproduce it.
  • We are legally required to. See “When we disclose data for legal reasons” below.
  • To protect the service or a person. For example, investigating an active security incident, or a credible threat of serious harm.

Access in those cases is limited to what is necessary. Beyond them, your content is handled by automated systems only. It is stored, backed up, and served back to you. We are a small team, and this is a statement of how we operate. We will update this policy if it ever changes.

Your content

When you use Causal you create content, which we call Customer Content: cards and the text inside them, files, images and videos you upload, links you save, canvases and layout, and comments you write on cards.

We store this so we can give it back to you. Uploaded files are held in a private storage bucket. They are never publicly accessible, and are served only through signed, time-limited links.

Other people’s information: your canvases may contain personal data about other people, such as a name in a note, a face in a photograph, or a client’s details in a document. We do not collect that data from those people directly, and we process it only on your instructions. If you put other people’s personal data into Causal, you are responsible for having a proper basis to do so, and for telling them if the law requires it.

Information we collect automatically

  • Product usage events. We record a deliberately narrow set of actions, such as signing in, creating a canvas, creating or deleting an item, uploading a file, and sharing or unsharing a canvas.
  • File metadata, not file contents. When you upload a file we record its type, extension and size. We do not send the contents of your files to our analytics provider.
  • Device and technical data, such as browser, operating system, screen size, referring page, and whether you are on the web or desktop app.
  • Approximate location. We derive an approximate city or country from your IP address. IP anonymisation is switched on, so your full IP address is not stored against your analytics profile. We do not collect precise or GPS location.
  • Error and crash diagnostics. When something breaks we log the error with structural context such as which canvas was affected and how many items were involved. These reports deliberately exclude the contents of your cards and files.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard request logs, including IP address, for security and reliability.

We do not use session recording or screen replay. We do not watch a video of your session, and we do not record your keystrokes or mouse movements.

Information from third parties

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address, name and profile picture with us, subject to the permissions you grant. We do not receive your Google password or gain access to any other Google service.

Data stored on your own device

Causal keeps a copy of your canvases in your browser’s local storage so the app loads instantly and keeps working through a brief connection drop. We also remember which canvas you had open. This lives on your device, not on our servers, and clearing your browser data or signing out removes it. It exists solely to deliver the app you asked for, and is never used for advertising.

The Causal Chrome extension

The Causal extension for Google Chrome saves things you find on the web into your canvases. Nothing from a page is sent to Causal until you choose a canvas and save. What you save becomes part of your content and is handled exactly as described above. Depending on what you save, that is:

  • A picture: the image file, and the address of the page it was on.
  • Highlighted text: the text, with the title and address of the page it came from.
  • A link: its address. Our servers then visit that address to fetch the page’s title, description and preview image, as they do for any link you save in Causal.
  • A post on X or Threads: the text of the post only.

To do this, the extension runs a small script on the pages you visit. That script remembers only where you last right-clicked, so it can find the picture you meant, and that position never leaves your browser. On X and Threads it also adds a save button to posts, and nothing from a post is sent anywhere until you click it. The extension does not record or send your browsing history.

The extension uses the Causal account you are signed in to in Chrome. It has no separate sign-in and never reads your sign-in cookies; Chrome attaches them to the extension’s requests to Causal, just as it does when you use the app.

In Chrome’s own storage, the extension keeps your list of canvases and any save still in progress, both cleared when you close the browser, and the canvas you last saved to, so it can offer it first next time.

Each save is recorded as a product usage event, like other actions in Causal: what kind of item it was (and, for a post, whether it came from X or Threads), whether it saved, and how long it took. These events never include addresses, titles or the text you saved.

Our use of information received through the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

04AI features

Causal has AI features: an assistant you can talk to about your canvases, and transcription for audio recordings. They run only when you use them. To work, they send the content involved to an AI provider, which uses it to produce the result.

The assistant

When you send the assistant a message, we send it to OpenRouter together with what the assistant needs to answer: the canvas you are on, any canvases or files you add to the conversation, the titles of your canvases, the conversation so far, and the pictures it looks at, including ones you add and snapshots it takes of your canvas. OpenRouter passes the request to the company that runs the AI model, which processes it to write the reply.

The assistant can also look things up. It can search the web through Tavily, which receives the search terms the assistant writes, and our servers can visit web pages and download images on its behalf.

We keep your conversations so you can come back to them. A conversation stays until you delete it or your account, and deleting a canvas does not delete the conversations about it. Pictures you add to a conversation are deleted with it. Snapshots of your canvas the assistant takes are kept until your account is deleted. When the assistant describes one of your images, we keep the description with that canvas so the image does not need describing again.

We record the text of each message you send to the assistant, up to 2,000 characters, in our product analytics, so we can see how the assistant is used and improve it. For the assistant’s replies we record only details such as which model answered, how long it took and what it cost, never what it said.

Transcribing audio

When you transcribe an audio recording, we send the recording to OpenRouter, which passes it to a speech-to-text model, and then send the transcript back through OpenRouter to tidy it up. The finished transcript is saved on your canvas as part of your content.

How AI providers receive your content

We send your pictures and recordings to AI providers as the files themselves, never as links to where they are stored. We do not use your content to train AI models. OpenRouter and the model providers it uses handle your content under their own terms.

AI apps you connect

You can connect an AI app, such as Claude, to your Causal account. When you do, you choose whether it can only read your canvases or also change them, and that access covers every canvas you own. The app then reads and changes your content when you ask it to, and sends it to its own AI provider. What happens to your content there is covered by that app’s privacy policy, not this one. You can disconnect an app at any time in Settings, under MCP.

09Security

Our main measures:

  • Encryption in transit. All traffic is served over HTTPS.
  • Encryption at rest. Your database and files are encrypted at rest by our infrastructure provider.
  • No public file URLs. Uploads go to a private bucket, served only through signed, time-limited links. Links used by public share pages expire within about 15 minutes so revoking a share takes effect quickly.
  • No passwords. We use one-time codes, magic links and Google sign-in, so there is no password of yours for us to leak.
  • Access controls. Database row-level security plus an explicit ownership check on every read and write, so one account cannot reach another account’s canvases.
  • Secret management. API keys and credentials are held in a managed secret store, never in our source code.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours as required, and notify you without undue delay where the risk is high.
