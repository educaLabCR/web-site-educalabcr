Changelog

  1. Nov 4, 2026

    Export from shared views

    Anyone you share a view with can now export pages and download files, singly or all at once.

    • New·: A menu in the share view offers Export and Download all.
    • New·: Right-click selected files in a share view to download just those.
    • New·: Several files download together as one zip, and pictures are included in page exports.
    • New·: Ask the assistant to add an item to a page's Resources.
    • Improved·: A space without a chosen icon now picks one from its title.
    • Fixed·: A page download retries when your connection drops.
    • Fixed·: The feedback card confirms once your message is sent.
  2. Oct 30, 2026

    Custom colours and feedback

    Pick your own colours, send feedback from inside the app, and enjoy a round of sharing improvements.

    • New·: Choose custom colours for your content.
    • New·: Send feedback to the team from inside the app.
    • Improved·: Sharing a view is smoother.
  3. Oct 29, 2026

    Guided tutorials

    New accounts now get short guided tutorials with a checklist in the sidebar, so you learn canvases, pages, boards and AI chat by doing.

    • New·: A tutorial checklist in the sidebar walks you through each space, one step at a time.
    • New·: Tips point at the control they explain and move on when you use it.
    • New·: Your starter page and canvas arrive ready to explore and do not count towards your limits.
    • Fixed·: A new board appears on screen straight away.
    • Fixed·: Typing an email without an ending such as .com now tells you what is missing at sign in.
  4. Oct 24, 2026

    Boards

    Plan a project in a dedicated board space, with tasks you can move through stages and view as a board, a list, a calendar or a roadmap.

    • New·: Create a board from the sidebar, the toolbar or by typing /kanban.
    • New·: Drag tasks between columns, and group them into lanes.
    • New·: Switch the same tasks between board, list, calendar and roadmap views.
    • New·: Ask the assistant to create and update tasks on a board for you.
    • New·: Export a canvas or page as a PNG, JPG, PDF or Markdown file from its menu in the sidebar.
    • Improved·: Usage now has its own section in settings.
    • Improved·: Suggest a feature and read the changelog from the feedback section in settings.
  5. Oct 17, 2026

    Shapes and Mermaid charts

    Draw lines, rectangles, ellipses, triangles and stars straight from the toolbar, and turn a code block into a Mermaid chart in any note or page.

    • New·: Pick a shape from the toolbar and drag it onto the canvas.
    • New·: Lines and arrows take end caps, so a connector can point, stop or both.
    • New·: Mermaid charts render in notes and pages. Start from a code block and the chart draws itself.
    • New·: Ask the assistant, or your own agent over MCP, to draw shapes and charts for you.
    • Fixed·: Switching canvases no longer shows a usage limit popup you have not reached.
    • Fixed·: A note opened full screen now follows dark mode.
  6. Oct 12, 2026

    Folders and model choice

    Group canvases and pages into folders in the sidebar, and choose which model answers you in AI chat.

    • New·: Create folders in the sidebar and drag canvases and pages into them.
    • New·: Pin a folder to keep it at the top of the sidebar.
    • New·: A model picker in AI chat, with new frontier models to choose from.
  7. Oct 8, 2026

    Pages and side by side views

    Pages are now top level spaces with a block editor, and a page or canvas can open beside the one you are on so you can work across both.

    • New·: Create a page on its own, next to your canvases in the sidebar.
    • New·: Embed canvases, files, images, links and audio inside a page.
    • New·: Open any canvas or page in a second view and drag things between the two.
    • New·: Share a single text file with its own read-only link.
    • Improved·: Drag blocks by their handle to reorder a note or page.
    • New·: New features now introduce themselves the first time you open the app after a release.
  8. Oct 1, 2026

    Save to Causal from Chrome

    The Chrome extension saves pictures, highlighted text, links and posts from X and Threads into the unsorted tray of the canvas you choose.

    • New·: Save with a right click, the toolbar icon or Alt+Shift+S.
    • Improved·: A picture dragged in from another web page now lands on the canvas.
    • Fixed·: Cut, paste into collections, multi-select and wheel zoom behave as expected again.
  9. Sep 25, 2026

    Video controls and easier resizing

    Videos play with proper controls, pictures resize from any side, and a whole selection resizes in one go.

    • New·: Video plays with its own controls, with a setting to autoplay muted on loop.
    • Improved·: Images and videos resize from every side and corner, keeping their shape.
    • New·: Drag a grip on a selection to resize everything in it, undone in one step.
    • New·: Make a note flat, with sharp corners and no shadow.
    • Improved·: The Stop button in AI chat now stops a reply straight away.
    • Fixed·: Long documents no longer jump to the top or drop keystrokes while you type.
  10. Sep 22, 2026

    Drawing and attachments

    Draw freehand on the canvas with a pen, brush, highlighter and eraser, and attach files and canvases to the cards they belong with.

    • New·: Freehand drawing with a pen, brush, highlighter and eraser.
    • New·: Attach a file or a canvas to a card. It sits in the card footer as a pill.
    • New·: Browse the template gallery on our website and open any template straight into a new canvas.
    • Fixed·: PDF previews open again in Safari 18.1.
  11. Sep 16, 2026

    The starter library

    Templates, prompt ideas and widget templates now live in one place in the right sidebar, so a new canvas never has to start blank.

    • New·: Preview a template on its own canvas before deciding to keep it.
    • New·: Prompt ideas for the assistant, drawn from what people ask it most.
    • New·: Widget templates, and copy and paste for widgets.
    • Improved·: New accounts get a short starting guide on the real controls instead of a set of popups.
  12. Sep 12, 2026

    Voice notes

    Record audio straight onto the canvas, play it back, and turn it into a transcript you can search and build on.

    • New·: Record into an audio card, or drop in an existing recording.
    • New·: Transcribe with one click. The transcript is saved on the card.
    • New·: The assistant and your own agent over MCP can transcribe audio too.
  13. Sep 9, 2026

    Invite friends, sign up anywhere

    Invite friends to earn more room on the free plan, and start your account from your phone.

    • New·: Each friend who joins from your invitation adds 20 notes, images and links to your free plan.
    • New·: Sign up from your phone and pick up on your computer.
    • Improved·: Every settings page has its own link.
  14. Sep 1, 2026

    Colours and pictures

    Colour swatches are now their own cards with a full colour picker, and the assistant sees the pictures on your canvas more clearly.

    • New·: A colour card with a name and its code in hex, RGB or HSL.
    • New·: Pick any colour with a hue, saturation and opacity picker, wherever colours appear.
    • New·: Copying an image puts the picture itself on your clipboard.
    • Improved·: The assistant places new content before creating it, so boards stay tidy.
    • Fixed·: Unsaved work now survives a refresh while saves are failing.
  15. Aug 31, 2026

    Widgets

    Ask the assistant for a small working tool, like a timer, tracker or checklist, and it builds it as a widget that lives on your canvas.

    • New·: Widgets keep their own state, so what you enter is still there next time.
    • New·: Park a widget in the unsorted tray until you need it.
    • Improved·: Widgets size themselves to fit what they show.
  16. Aug 29, 2026

    Pro, the Mac app and MCP

    A Pro plan for when you outgrow the free one, a Mac app that keeps itself up to date, and MCP so Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and VS Code can work with your canvases.

    • New·: Pro removes the limits on notes, files and widgets and raises your AI allowance.
    • New·: Download Causal for Mac from settings or our website.
    • New·: Connect your own agent over MCP to read and build on any canvas.
    • New·: Duplicate a canvas, with everything inside it.
  17. Aug 23, 2026

    Canvas AI

    The assistant in the right sidebar reads your whole canvas, including documents and pictures, and builds on it with you.

    • New·: It reads the documents and sees the pictures on your canvas.
    • New·: It searches across every canvas you own, not just the one you are on.
    • New·: It writes documents, draws arrows, groups things and moves them between boards.
    • New·: Anything the assistant changes can be undone.
    • New·: Your chat history is saved, so you can return to a conversation.
  18. Aug 18, 2026

    A new sidebar and shortcuts

    Your canvases now sit in a tree in the left sidebar, every tool has a single key shortcut, and things snap into line as you drag them.

    • New·: A sidebar tree of your canvases, with a breadcrumb trail above the board.
    • New·: Single key shortcuts for every tool, and click or drag to place.
    • New·: Hold the spacebar and drag to move around the canvas.
    • New·: Smart alignment snaps a dragged card to the edges and centres around it. Turn it on in settings.
    • Fixed·: Text typed just before closing your laptop is no longer lost.
    • Fixed·: Large pasted images no longer disappear.
